Data Deletion Instructions

To request deletion of locally held data, email holger@mobilis.de and include only the Instagram handle to identify the account. Never send a password, access token, backup code, or authentication code.

After verification of the request, local access tokens, account identifiers, comment or message snapshots, reply drafts, and metrics associated with the account will be removed unless retention is required for security, audit, dispute handling, or applicable law.
