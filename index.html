AgentOS Social Content OS — Privacy Notice

Operator: Holger Rannow · holger@mobilis.de

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Scope

AgentOS Social Content OS connects only Instagram professional accounts that an authorized account administrator explicitly grants access to.

Data processed

The service may process Instagram account identifiers, granted permissions and access tokens stored in a protected secret store; media and captions selected for publishing; comments and messages when those functions are enabled; and account or media insights.

Purposes

Data is used only to manage the authorized Instagram presence, prepare and publish approved content, moderate interactions, and verify platform readback and insights. Data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Access credentials are kept separately in a protected secret store. Operational records are retained only as long as needed for the authorized service, security, audit, recovery, or applicable legal obligations. Access can be revoked in Meta or Instagram at any time.

Meta

Meta and Instagram also process data under their own privacy policies. Their processing is governed by their terms and policies.

Your rights and deletion

For access, correction, objection, revocation, or deletion requests, contact holger@mobilis.de. See the data deletion instructions.
